# Skill catalog

Choose a skill by the result you need. Each name opens its complete instructions.

## Research and data

| Skill | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| [source-brief](skills/source-brief/SKILL.md) | Answer a research question from supplied or accessible sources, with citations, disagreements and gaps in the evidence. |
| [action-items](skills/action-items/SKILL.md) | Extract decisions, owners, deadlines and open questions from meeting notes, transcripts or correspondence. |
| [analyze-data](skills/analyze-data/SKILL.md) | Explain a change in tabular data, reconcile group contributions and check rates, missing values and denominators. |
| [data-files](skills/data-files/SKILL.md) | Create, convert or check CSV, JSON, Markdown and plain text while preserving the meaning of the input. |

## Software development

| Skill | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
