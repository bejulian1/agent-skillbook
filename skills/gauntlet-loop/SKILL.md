# Iterate on a result with review

Agree on observable acceptance criteria from the request. Choose a review budget that fits the user's time and resource limits. Produce a first result that a reviewer can exercise.

For each round, supply the deliverable and acceptance criteria to an independent reviewer when delegation is available and authorized. Give reviewers a bounded area and avoid concurrent edits to the same files. If working alone, carry out a separate inspection pass and label it as self-review.

Have the reviewer exercise the result and identify specific gaps with evidence. Prioritize the findings, make the fixes and repeat the affected checks on the integrated result. Stop when the agreed criteria hold and no material findings remain, or when the budget ends. Report remaining findings and the checks actually performed.
